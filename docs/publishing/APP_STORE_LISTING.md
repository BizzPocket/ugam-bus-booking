## Description (4000 char max)
```
Ugam Booking is the easiest way to join group bus tours and yatras run by Ugam Foj.

Browse every upcoming trip, see exactly what each tour includes, and send your seat request straight from your phone — no phone tag, no confusion.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Discover upcoming tours — dates, route, and details at a glance
• Open any trip to see the full itinerary and seating
• Request your seats in a few taps
• Track all your requests in one place, so you always know where you stand

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Clean, simple screens designed for everyone in the group
• See trip details before you commit
• Your requests stay organised — no more scrolling through chats to find what you booked

Ugam Booking keeps the whole journey — from picking a tour to confirming your seat — in one tidy app.

Run by Ugam Foj / DEVAM. Learn more at devam.org.
```
