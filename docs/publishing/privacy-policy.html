Privacy Policy — Ugam Booking

Last updated: 27 May 2026

Ugam Booking (“the app”) is operated by Ugam Foj / DEVAM (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account & contact details — your name and phone number, so we can identify your booking requests and contact you about a trip.
  • Booking information — the tours you view and the seat requests you submit, so we can process and track them.
  • Device contacts (optional) — if you grant permission, the app matches names from your device’s contacts to incoming bookings so you can recognise who booked. Contact matching happens on your device; we do not upload your full address book.

How we use your information

  • To show you upcoming tours and trip details.
  • To create, process, and track your seat requests.
  • To contact you about a booking, including via WhatsApp when you choose to continue a request there.

How your information is stored

Booking and account data is stored securely on our backend provider (Supabase) and transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or third-party tracking. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app (e.g. our backend host) and only as required to operate the booking service.

WhatsApp

The app can open WhatsApp to continue a booking conversation. Anything you send in WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the contacts permission at any time in your device settings; the app still works without it.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us at the address below.

Children

The app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
